## Research Interests

## Current Work

### Learning to Match with Two-Sided Acceptance (working title)

M.S. thesis in progress, advised by [Prof. Min-hwan Oh](https://minoh.io). I am developing a bandit algorithm that learns both sides' preferences from sequential feedback to increase mutually accepted matches, motivated by ride-hailing. The work uses bilinear matching bandits and maximum-weight matching, and establishes an expected cumulative regret bound.

## Previous Research Experience

From December 2021 to September 2022, I completed a lab internship with Prof. Ji-hyun Lee in Yonsei University's Department of Mathematics. I studied SIR and SEIR models and used MATLAB simulations to explore how vaccination, social distancing, and quarantine affect disease spread.
