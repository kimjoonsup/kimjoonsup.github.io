**Works in Progress**

**Advisor:** [Prof. Min-hwan Oh](https://minoh.io)

## Overview

This work investigates preference learning and stable matching in markets where the number of potential partners exceeds the available interaction horizon. With limited opportunities to interact, participants must learn about potential matches while exploring only a small part of the market.

## Model

I have formulated an infinite-market model incorporating **latent participant quality**, **pair-specific preferences**, and **noisy feedback from both sides**. The model describes learning when exhaustive exploration of potential partners is unavailable.

## Current Focus

I define regret relative to a proposed limiting stable-matching benchmark to evaluate learning with limited partner exploration. The work is in progress, with a focus on preference learning and the benchmark used to assess matching performance in large markets.
